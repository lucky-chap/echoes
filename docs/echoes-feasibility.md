## Independent dates and geography

- Ghanaian highlife’s first returned music candidate was Manu Dibango. MusicBrainz returned in-window works including *Deliverance* (1987). Qloo’s geographical metadata identified Cameroon, not Ghana. This is taste affinity, not evidence of the resident’s personal familiarity or Ghanaian origin.
- The same profile’s first film was *Green Book* (Qloo release year 2018; countries included China, South Korea, and United States). Its first TV result was *The Get Down* (2016; United States). Those Qloo dates are outside the 1983–1995 window and were **not independently TMDB-verified**.
- British rock’s first returned music candidate was The Who. MusicBrainz supplied *My Generation* (1965-12-03), inside the 1958–1970 window. This does not establish personal preference; caregiver review remains necessary.
- American soul’s first TV result was *Star* (Qloo release year 2016; United States). Country alignment alone is not enough: the year is outside the 1957–1969 window, and no independent TMDB check was available.
- `TMDB_API_KEY` was not configured. Film/TV cross-domain catalogue coverage was measured where Qloo responded, but independent film/TV date verification was not.
- MusicBrainz browse is bounded to the first 100 release groups. A missing in-window work in a truncated catalogue is not proof that none exists.

## Decisions informed by this audit

1. Retain exact favourites as candidates; do not depend entirely on adjacent discoveries.
2. Never equate a timeout or HTTP failure with missing catalogue coverage.
3. Preserve independent per-item year gates and reject ambiguous identities/remakes.
4. Use one bounded broader-discovery retry when too few candidates survive.
5. If verification fails, offer a non-media plan with clear limitations rather than inventing a cultural item.
6. Label Qloo outputs as suggestions, not personal memories. Geographical relevance remains evidence-dependent and caregiver-reviewed.
7. Offer media-provider checks where identifiers are supplied, otherwise explicit search instructions. Playback itself was not verified by this audit.

## Remaining feasibility work

Configure TMDB and repeat film/TV verification; retry failed Qloo domains under stable networking; test a larger, culturally varied set; verify more actual recordings on the intended tablets/regions. These findings support a bounded pilot, not claims of universal cultural coverage or clinical effectiveness.
